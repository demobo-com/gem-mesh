LEASE OF OFFICE PREMISES

Dated 2 March 2026

Landlord: ACME HOLDINGS LIMITED, 1 Embarcadero Center, San Francisco, CA 94111
Tenant: NORTHSTAR LEGAL LLP, 500 Howard Street, San Francisco, CA 94105
Guarantors: Elena Ruiz and Marcus Bell, senior partners of the Tenant

Premises: Third floor, 220 Market Street, San Francisco (approx. 11,400 sq ft)

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 In this Lease, 'Premises' means the third floor of 220 Market Street, San Francisco, shown edged red on Plan 1; 'Building' means 220 Market Street; 'Term' means five years from the Commencement Date; 'Commencement Date' means 2 March 2026.

2. DEMISE
2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for the Term together with the rights in Schedule 1 and subject to the rights reserved in Schedule 2.

3. RENT
3.1 The Tenant shall pay the initial annual Rent of $684,000 yearly in advance in one sum on the Commencement Date and on each anniversary of it. No apportionment shall be made for any part year.

4. RENT REVIEW
4.1 The Rent shall be reviewed on the date falling fourteen (14) months after the Commencement Date and every twelve (12) months thereafter by reference to the Consumer Price Index, provided that no single increase shall exceed nine per cent (9%) and the Rent shall never decrease.

5. DEPOSIT
5.1 On completion the Tenant shall pay a deposit equal to twelve months' Rent, to be held by the Landlord in its general account without interest and returned within ninety (90) days after the end of the Term less any sums the Landlord in its discretion considers due.

6. SERVICE CHARGE
6.1 The Tenant shall pay 14.5% of the Service Costs set out in Schedule 4, payable quarterly in advance. The Landlord may vary the percentage at any time by written notice without giving reasons.

7. INSURANCE
7.1 The Landlord shall insure the Building for its full reinstatement value. The Tenant shall pay the Insurance Rent within fourteen (14) days of demand, including any commission received by the Landlord.

8. USE
8.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as offices for a law practice and for no other purpose. The Tenant shall not hold events attended by more than forty (40) people.

9. CATERING
9.1 All food and beverages served at the Premises for meetings or events shall be supplied exclusively by the Landlord's nominated caterer at the caterer's standard rates.

10. OPENING HOURS
10.1 The Building is open 07:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. Access outside those hours requires 48 hours' notice and payment of the Landlord's out-of-hours charge of $180 per hour.

11. REPAIR
11.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good and substantial repair and condition, fair wear and tear excepted, including all plant and machinery exclusively serving the Premises.

12. DECORATION
12.1 The Tenant shall redecorate the interior in the third year of the Term and in the last three months of the Term, in colours approved by the Landlord.

13. ALTERATIONS
13.1 The Tenant shall not make structural alterations. Internal non-structural alterations require the Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, and must be carried out by the Landlord's approved contractors.

14. SIGNAGE
14.1 The Tenant may display its name only on the Building directory board in the Landlord's standard lettering. No signage may be visible from the street.

15. ASSIGNMENT
15.1 The Tenant shall not assign the whole of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Underletting of part is prohibited.

16. CHANGE OF CONTROL
16.1 Any change in the persons who control the Tenant shall be treated as an assignment requiring the Landlord's consent.

17. BREAK OPTION
17.1 The Landlord may terminate this Lease on the second anniversary of the Commencement Date by giving the Tenant not less than six (6) months' written notice. The Tenant shall have no corresponding right to terminate.

18. RELOCATION
18.1 The Landlord may on three (3) months' notice require the Tenant to relocate to other premises in the Building of similar size. The Tenant shall bear its own relocation costs.

19. LANDLORD'S ACCESS
19.1 The Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable times on 24 hours' notice to inspect, repair or show the Premises to prospective tenants or purchasers, and at any time in an emergency.

20. UTILITIES
20.1 The Tenant shall pay for all electricity, telecommunications and other utilities consumed at the Premises, metered separately where possible.

21. COMPLIANCE
21.1 The Tenant shall comply with all laws relating to its use of the Premises, including fire safety and accessibility requirements.

22. INDEMNITY
22.1 The Tenant shall indemnify the Landlord against all losses arising from any breach of the Tenant's covenants or from the Tenant's use of the Premises.

23. LANDLORD'S LIABILITY
23.1 The Landlord shall not be liable to the Tenant for any interruption to services, loss of access or damage to the Tenant's property, howsoever caused, including by the Landlord's negligence.

24. RENT SUSPENSION
24.1 If the Premises are damaged by an insured risk and made unfit for use, the Rent shall be suspended for up to six (6) months only.

25. YIELD UP
25.1 At the end of the Term the Tenant shall yield up the Premises with vacant possession, remove its fixtures and reinstate any alterations if the Landlord requires.

26. FORFEITURE
26.1 If any Rent is unpaid seven (7) days after it is due, or the Tenant breaches any covenant, the Landlord may re-enter the Premises and this Lease shall end.

27. INTEREST
27.1 Any sum not paid when due shall bear interest at 8% above the Prime Rate from the due date until payment.

28. NOTICES
28.1 Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand or by registered post to the addresses in this Lease. Email notices are not valid.

29. GUARANTEE
29.1 The Tenant's obligations are guaranteed by its two senior partners personally, jointly and severally, for the whole Term and any renewal.

30. GOVERNING LAW
30.1 This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of California and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of San Francisco.
