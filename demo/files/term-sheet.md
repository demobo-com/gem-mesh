# Summary of Terms: Series A Preferred Stock Financing of Corvid Labs, Inc.

October 24, 2026. CONFIDENTIAL.

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed Series A financing of Corvid Labs, Inc. Except for the
sections titled Confidentiality, No-Shop and Expenses, it is not binding on either party.

## Key terms

| Term | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Amount raised | $40,000,000 |
| Pre-money valuation | $160,000,000 (fully diluted) |
| Post-money valuation | $200,000,000 |
| Option pool | 10% of the post-money capitalization, unallocated, included in the pre-money |
| Liquidation preference | 1x, non-participating |
| Board seats for Northwind | 1 |

## Offering

- **Issuer:** Corvid Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company").
- **Security:** Series A Preferred Stock (the "Series A").
- **Investors:** Northwind Ventures Fund IV, L.P. ("Northwind"), $30,000,000, and other investors acceptable to Northwind
  and the Company, $10,000,000.
- **Price per share:** the price that gives the pre-money valuation above on a fully diluted basis, including the option
  pool.
- **Closing:** a single closing, expected within 30 days of signing this term sheet.

## Pro forma capitalization (fully diluted, after closing)

| Founders (two) | 51.0% |

## Use of proceeds

- About $22,000,000 for compute for the Corvid-2 training run.
- About $12,000,000 for hiring, mainly research and enterprise sales, over 18 months.
- The rest for general corporate purposes and working capital.

## Terms of the Series A

- **Dividends:** 6% per year, non-cumulative, only when declared by the Board, and in preference to the Common Stock.
- **Liquidation preference:** on a sale or liquidation, each Series A share receives the greater of (a) 1x its original
  purchase price plus declared and unpaid dividends, or (b) what it would receive if converted to Common Stock. The
  Series A does not participate further.
- **Conversion:** at the holder's option at any time, one Series A share to one share of Common Stock, subject to the
  anti-dilution adjustment below. Automatic on a firm-commitment IPO of at least $100,000,000.
- **Anti-dilution:** broad-based weighted average.
- **Voting:** the Series A votes with the Common Stock on an as-converted basis.
- **Protective provisions:** standard; consent of a majority of the Series A is needed to change the Series A rights,
  create a senior or equal security, sell the Company, or change the size of the Board.
- **Pay-to-play:** none.

## Board

The Board will have three members: two elected by the Common Stock (initially the two founders) and one elected by
Northwind.

## Investor rights

- **Information rights:** annual audited and quarterly unaudited financial statements, and an annual budget, for each
  investor holding at least $2,000,000 of Series A (a "Major Investor").
- **Pro rata rights:** each Major Investor may buy its pro rata share of future equity offerings.
- **Registration rights:** standard demand, piggyback and S-3 rights.
- **Drag-along:** the founders and the Series A will vote for a sale approved by the Board and a majority of the
  Series A.

## Transfers

- **Right of first refusal:** the Company first, then the Major Investors, may buy any shares a founder proposes to
  sell, on the same terms.
- **Co-sale:** if a founder sells shares to a third party, each Major Investor may sell a pro rata part of its shares
  in the same sale.
- **Lock-up:** each holder agrees not to sell for 180 days after an IPO.

## Conditions to closing

- Satisfactory completion of Northwind's financial, legal and technical due diligence, including a review of the
  Company's model evaluations and compute contracts.
- Definitive agreements drafted by Northwind's counsel and acceptable to both parties.
- Each founder and employee has signed the Company's standard invention assignment and confidentiality agreement.
- No material adverse change in the Company's business before closing.
- Approval of Northwind's investment committee.

## Company matters

- **Key-person insurance:** the Company will hold $2,000,000 of life insurance on each founder, payable to the
  Company, within 90 days of closing.
- **Employee agreements:** every new employee and consultant signs the standard invention assignment and
  confidentiality agreement before starting.
- **Employee stock options:** granted from the pool at fair market value, vesting over four years with a one-year
  cliff. Grants above 1% of the fully diluted capitalization need Board approval, including the Northwind director.
- **Compute contracts:** any new compute commitment above $5,000,000 a year needs Board approval.
- **Indemnification:** the Company will indemnify its directors to the fullest extent Delaware law allows and carry
  directors and officers insurance of at least $5,000,000.
- **Management rights:** Northwind receives a standard management rights letter.

## Other terms

- **Founder vesting:** four years from the original grant, with 12 months already credited. Full acceleration on a
  sale followed by termination without cause (double trigger).
- **Option pool:** 10% of the post-money capitalization, reserved for future grants.
- **No-shop:** for 30 days the Company will not solicit or negotiate another equity financing.
- **Expenses:** the Company pays Northwind's legal fees up to $50,000 at closing.
- **Confidentiality:** the Company will not disclose these terms to anyone except its officers, directors, key service
  providers and other potential investors in this financing, without Northwind's consent.
- **Expiration:** this offer expires on October 31, 2026, if not signed by the Company.
- **Governing law:** Delaware.
