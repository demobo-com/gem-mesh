# Demo script: "Off the grid at AGI House"

**The founder:** Priya, CEO of **Corvid Labs**, a frontier model lab competing with Anthropic and OpenAI
**The investor:** Dana, a partner at **Northwind Ventures**
**The rule:** nothing goes to Claude, ChatGPT or any cloud AI. They're her competitors.
**Devices:** Pixel (Gemma E2B) · laptop (Gemma 12B) · workstation (Gemma 26B, thinking)
**Input:** voice only, spoken to the Pixel.

| Device | Gemma | What only it does here |
|---|---|---|
| Pixel | E2B | **Ears and eyes.** It hears her voice and reads photos, instantly, with no network. |
| Laptop | 12B | **The analyst.** It reads long private documents faithfully, compares them, and writes well. |
| Workstation | 26B + thinking | **The strategist.** It reasons through deal math and builds the final deliverable step by step. |

## Hook (20 seconds)

> "I run an AI lab. We compete with Anthropic and OpenAI. So here's my problem: I can't paste my benchmarks, my burn
> rate or my term sheet into Claude or ChatGPT. That would hand them to my competitors.
>
> Today I'm raising our round at AGI House. And of course, the Wi-Fi is down and I have one bar of signal."

## Scene 1: arriving at AGI House (phone only)

**Gemma E2B on the Pixel's TPU: it hears and sees.**

*Show the phone: Wi-Fi off, one bar, Airplane Mode on.*

> "Ten minutes before the meeting. No internet I can count on. Just my phone."

🎙️ **Priya:** *"Give me our key numbers. Burn, runway and revenue."*

📱 *Answers instantly from notes stored on the phone:* **$3.2M a month burn · 5 months runway · $14M ARR.**

> "My most sensitive numbers, recalled by voice, with no network."

*Dana's associate hands over a printed term sheet. Photograph it.*

🎙️ **Priya:** *"What's the raise, the valuation and the option pool?"*

📱 **$40M raise · $160M pre-money · 10% option pool.** *Badge: Pixel · E2B · on device.*

> "The smallest Gemma is the one with ears and eyes. My voice and that term sheet never left my pocket."

## Scene 2: in the meeting room (phone and laptop)

**Gemma 12B on the laptop: it reads, compares and writes.**

> "My phone and laptop connect directly to each other. Nothing goes out to the internet."

*Show the laptop: connected to the phone, no internet.*

**Beat 1: the tough question**

> "Dana asks the question every investor asks an AI lab: *'How do you actually stack up against Claude and GPT?'*"

🎙️ **Priya:** *"From our internal eval report, give me three talking points on how our model compares with Claude and
GPT, with the numbers."*

📱 *Handed to the laptop: Laptop · 12B.* Three sharp talking points, quoting the private eval report.

> "Imagine pasting your benchmark results against Claude… into Claude. The eval report is far too long for the phone,
> so the phone handed it to the laptop."

**Beat 2: the negotiation**

*Dana goes to the whiteboard and sweetens the deal: **$180M pre · 1.5x participating preference · 2 board seats**.
Photograph it.*

🎙️ **Priya:** *"Compare the whiteboard with my negotiation notes. Are we inside our walk-away? Draft a counter."*

📱 *The phone reads the photo, and the laptop does the rest:*

| Term | Whiteboard | Walk-away | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-money | $180M | at least $150M | ✅ |
| Preference | 1.5x participating | 1x non-participating only | ❌ |
| Board seats | 2 | at most 1 | ❌ |

…plus a warm, firm counter Priya can read aloud.

> "The phone saw the whiteboard. The laptop checked it against my secret walk-away and wrote my counter, in the room,
> offline. I tell Dana I'll get back to her tonight."

## Scene 3: back at the office (phone, laptop and workstation)

**Gemma 26B with thinking on the workstation: it reasons and builds.**

> "Back at the office, the workstation joins my mesh. Dana raised my valuation by $20 million. Is that a better deal?
> That isn't a lookup. It's math across clauses."

🎙️ **Priya:** *"Northwind raised our valuation. Is it actually better? Using the term sheet and my negotiation notes,
show me what common gets if we're acquired for two hundred million."*

📱 *Workstation · 26B · thinking…* The judges watch it reason. Then:

> **No. The higher valuation is a trap.**
> - **Original** ($160M pre, 1x non-participating): Northwind takes $40M, and **common gets $160M**.
> - **Whiteboard** ($180M pre, 1.5x participating): Northwind takes $60M first, then 18% of the remaining $140M,
>   about $85M in total. **Common gets about $115M.**
> - The "better" offer costs founders and employees **about $45M, or 28%**. Trade the valuation bump for
>   1x non-participating.

> "The phone answered quick questions. The laptop spotted which terms broke my rules. Only the workstation could work
> out what they cost."

🎙️ **Priya:** *"Write a one-page memo for our board and lawyers with all of this, and stamp it confidential."*

📱 *An approval card appears. Tap yes, and **northwind-memo.pdf** lands on the phone, stamped CONFIDENTIAL.*

## Close (15 seconds)

> "At AGI House with no internet: my phone heard me and read the term sheet. My laptop answered the investor and
> drafted my counter. Back at the office, the workstation found a $45 million trap and wrote the board memo.
>
> Three sizes of Gemma, each doing what it's best at, on hardware I own. Not one byte went to my competitors."

## Props and numbers (they must match the script)

| Item | File in the phone's workspace | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Notes on the phone | `key-numbers.md` | Burn $3.2M a month · cash $16M → 5 months runway · ARR $14M |
| Printed term sheet | `term-sheet.md` (print it), then a photo of the printout; stand-in `term-sheet-photo.jpg` | $40M raise · $160M pre · 10% option pool · 1x non-participating · 1 board seat |
| Whiteboard | a photo; stand-in `whiteboard.jpg` | $180M pre · 1.5x participating · 2 board seats |
| Negotiation notes | `negotiation-notes.md` | Pre-money at least $150M · 1x non-participating only · at most 1 investor board seat · pool at most 12% |
| Internal eval report | `eval-report.md` | Corvid's private benchmark results versus Claude and GPT, several pages long |

The four documents and the two stand-in photos are in `demo/files/`, so **Settings → Demo files** puts them on the
phone. Use the stand-ins to rehearse, or on stage if a live photo comes out badly: attach them with **paperclip → Files**,
which keeps their names. Attach each photo with the paperclip in the same message as the question: the paperclip
writes the photo's name (`photo-20261024-…jpg`, or `whiteboard.jpg` for a stand-in) into the message box, which is how
the model knows which photo to look at. The microphone also writes into the message box,
so glance at the words before sending: the document names below must come through as said.

## Why each scene lands on its device

The router picks the device by the size of the prompt plus the conversation, about 4 characters per token
(`policy/tiers.yaml`): up to 1,500 tokens stays on the phone, above that goes to the laptop, above 3,000 prefers the
workstation. A document goes with the prompt when it is named, so say its name as written: "key numbers", "eval report",
"negotiation notes", "term sheet".

| Prompt | Documents attached | Tokens | Device |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key numbers | `key-numbers.md` | ~600 | Phone (and Airplane Mode leaves only the phone) |
| Term sheet photo | none (the phone looks at the photo) | ~600 | Phone |
| Eval report talking points | `eval-report.md` | ~2,100 | Laptop |
| Whiteboard vs. negotiation notes | `negotiation-notes.md` | ~2,000 in a fresh chat | Laptop (the workstation isn't there yet) |
| Is it actually better? | `term-sheet.md`, `negotiation-notes.md` | ~3,500 in a fresh chat | Workstation |
| Board memo | none (follows the previous answer) | ~3,500 | Workstation |

Each line holds on its own, in a fresh chat, so the bench version routes the same way. Checked by
`clients/android/app/src/test/java/com/demobo/mesh/route/AgiHouseDemoTest.kt`. If you edit the documents, keep the eval
report and the negotiation notes above 6,000 characters each, and the term sheet plus the notes above 12,000.

**Answer key, if a judge checks the math:**

- Original: $40M on $160M pre gives $200M post, so Northwind owns 20%. At a $200M sale, the 1x preference ($40M) and
  converting (20% = $40M) are the same. **Common gets $160M.**
- Whiteboard: $40M on $180M pre gives $220M post, so Northwind owns 18.2%. With 1.5x participating they take $60M
  first, then 18.2% of the remaining $140M ($25.5M), **$85.5M in total**. **Common gets $114.5M.**
- The difference is $45.5M, or 28.4% less for common.
