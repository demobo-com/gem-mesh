# Series A negotiation notes: Northwind

CONFIDENTIAL. Founders only. Do not forward, print or share with any investor. Last edited October 23, 2026.

## The goal

Close a $40M Series A by December 31, 2026, with clean terms and founder control of the board. Valuation matters less
than structure: a clean $150M beats a messy $180M. We need the money to finish Corvid-2's training run in Q1 2027.

## Walk-away terms (hard limits)

If the final terms break any of these, we do not sign. We go to Harborline instead.

| Term | Walk-away | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-money valuation | at least $150M | Below this, the founders drop under 50% after the option pool. |
| Liquidation preference | 1x non-participating only. Never participating, never above 1x. | Participating preferred takes money twice, before and alongside the common. It hurts employees most in a mid-size sale. |
| Investor board seats | at most 1 for Northwind | The board must stay 2 common to 1 investor. Two Northwind seats on a five-person board would take control away from the founders. |
| Option pool | at most 12% of the post-money, inside the pre-money | Every point above 10% comes out of the founders' pockets. |
| Anti-dilution | broad-based weighted average only. No full ratchet. | A full ratchet would wipe out the common in a down round. |
| No-shop | at most 30 days | We have 5 months of runway; a long no-shop is leverage against us. |
| Founder vesting | no reset; keep the 12 months credited | Resetting vesting is a signal the investor doesn't trust the founders. |
| Pay-to-play | none | |

## Targets (what a great outcome looks like)

- Pre-money $170M or more.
- 1x non-participating preference.
- One Northwind board seat, plus one independent director chosen together, later.
- Option pool at 10%.
- Pro rata rights for Northwind are fine.
- Investor legal fees capped at $50K.

## Trades we would accept

- **Give up valuation for clean structure.** We take $160M flat if the preference stays 1x non-participating and the
  board stays 2 to 1.
- **Board observer instead of a second seat.** If Dana wants more oversight, offer a non-voting observer seat.
- **Bigger pro rata.** Offer Northwind super pro rata in the next round (up to 1.5x its share) instead of a richer
  preference.
- **Faster close.** We can sign within 10 days if the terms are clean. That's worth more to Dana's partnership than the
  structure.
- **Information rights.** Monthly financials instead of quarterly, if that helps.

## Never say out loud

- Our runway is 5 months. If Northwind learns we're short on cash, they will push structure.
- Harborline's valuation is likely around $150M, lower than Northwind's. Only say "we have another term sheet coming".
- Our walk-away numbers on this page.

## Our alternatives (BATNA)

- **Harborline Capital.** Term sheet expected next week. Likely $150M pre, 1x non-participating, one board seat.
  Slower process, about 6 weeks to close, but clean.
- **Granite Peak Partners.** Interested, but their partnership meets only monthly. Next meeting is November 18.
- **Extend runway.** Cut the Corvid-2 run to a smaller model and stretch the cash to 8 months. Last resort; it would
  cost us the lead on long context.

## About Dana and Northwind

- Dana is a general partner at Northwind Ventures and led its last three AI infrastructure deals.
- Pattern: anchors high on valuation, then asks for structure in exchange. In her last two deals she raised the
  headline price and added a participating preference at the same time.
- Northwind's Fund IV is two years old and needs a breakout AI deal. They want this one.
- Dana responds well to data and speed, badly to pressure.

## What to push back on, if it comes up

1. **Participating preference.** "We're happy to talk about valuation, but we only do 1x non-participating. It keeps
   the team aligned with you in every outcome."
2. **A second board seat.** "We'd love your oversight. Let's do one seat plus a board observer, and add an independent
   director together after the next round."
3. **Bigger option pool.** "Our hiring plan needs 10% for the next 18 months. We'll share the plan."
4. **Longer no-shop.** "We can move fast. Thirty days is plenty, and we can sign within ten if the terms are clean."

## Questions to ask Dana

- What does Northwind need from this deal to call it a success in three years?
- Who else at Northwind has to approve, and when does the partnership meet?
- How would Northwind support Corvid-2: compute partners, enterprise introductions, recruiting?
- Will Northwind lead the next round, or just take its pro rata?

## How we'll answer the tough questions

- **"How do you compare with Claude and GPT?"** Use the latest internal eval report. Lead with cost and long context,
  not raw benchmark rank.
- **"Why won't the big labs crush you?"** Price: we're about 6x cheaper per token at similar quality. Focus: long
  documents for regulated industries, where customers can't send data to a frontier lab.
- **"What's your burn?"** "About $3M a month, mostly compute, and ARR is growing 18% a month." Don't volunteer the
  runway.
